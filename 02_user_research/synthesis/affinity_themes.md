# Synthesis: Thematic Pain Points

## Process

Every quote, hesitation, assist, workaround, and end of session answer from `raw_notes/P1_notes.md` and `P2_notes.md` was written as a separate note, tagged with participant and task (for example `P1 / T2`), and grouped by the underlying problem. Groups were then compared against the heuristic evaluation and accessibility audit. Because there were only 2 participants, a problem seen with one participant was kept as a theme when it was severe or the expert review confirmed it.

## Themes

### Theme 1: "Where do I see the time?" Class details are hard to read

**Participants:** P1, P2
**Tasks affected:** T2
**Description:** The novice could not find meeting times on her own and needed help, the only assisted task in the study. Even the experienced participant named Task 2 as the hardest because it takes a long time to find a section that fits her schedule.

**Supporting quotes and observations:**
* "Where do i see the time for the class" (P1)
* Confidence 2 of 5 after Task 2 (P1)
* Hardest task: "takes longer to find a class that works with my schedule and with other courses" (P2)

**Mental model mismatch:** Students expect class times to be the most visible fact in a result. Instead, days are shown as tiny gray letter boxes (S M T W T F S) with a highlighted letter, mixed into a cell with Type and Building text that is often cut off.

**Breakdown point:** Reading the Meeting Times column.

**Related findings:** HE06, HE11, HE13 (new), HE07; A05

---

### Theme 2: "Why do I have to click so many buttons?" Registration is spread across too many tools and steps

**Participants:** P1, P2
**Tasks affected:** T3, End
**Description:** Neither participant checked eligibility inside Register for Classes. P1 opened CatTracks in a separate tab and called going back and forth "a chore." P2 asks an advisor instead. P1 also questioned why one class takes so many steps (search, Add, choose an action, Submit).

**Supporting quotes and observations:**
* "Why do i have to click so many buttons" (P1)
* Hardest part: "going back and forth between CatTracks" (P1)
* Would "merge CatTracks and the registration portal" (P1)
* Checks prerequisites with an advisor (P2)

**Mental model mismatch:** Students expect one place to find a class, see whether they can take it, and register. The system splits this across Register for Classes, CatTracks, and advisors.

**Breakdown point:** Checking prerequisites (T3).

**Related findings:** HE10, HE14 (new), HE01, HE08

---

### Theme 3: Clicking Add isn't registering, and students learn that the hard way

**Participants:** P1, P2
**Tasks affected:** T3
**Description:** The first time P1 registered, she thought Add was all she had to click, and only realized otherwise when she spotted the Submit button. She also hesitated at choosing the "Enrolled" option before submitting. P2 knows the rule now and checks the bottom right corner, which shows the rule is learned, not communicated.

**Supporting quotes and observations:**
* Thought Add was all she had to click on her first registration (P1)
* Didn't know it worked until she found Submit and checked (P1)
* "Adds to schedule but doesn't register"; confirms in the bottom right corner (P2)

**Mental model mismatch:** Add works like a shopping cart that should finish the job. In reality it only stages the class until Submit.

**Breakdown point:** After clicking Add.

**Related findings:** HE01, HE04, HE08, HE11

---

### Theme 4: Experienced students route around the system

**Participants:** P2
**Tasks affected:** All
**Description:** The experienced participant completed every task quickly and without hesitation, but said she doesn't normally use the registration portal at all. She uses the Schedule Builder and her advisor, and the one thing she'd change is having to use the portal. Her smooth session reflects workarounds, not a smooth system.

**Supporting observations:**
* "Does not use registration portal, uses Schedule Builder" (P2)
* Checks prerequisites through an advisor (P2)
* One thing she'd change: using the registration portal (P2)

**Mental model mismatch:** The portal is meant to be the main registration tool, but an experienced user treats it as a last step to avoid.

**Related findings:** Supports the institutional cost in the problem statement (advising load).

---

### Theme 5: "Too much on my phone"

**Participants:** P1
**Tasks affected:** Warm up, all
**Description:** P1 started on her iPhone but switched to a computer because the page was too much on a small screen.

**Supporting quote:**
* "I had to switch to the computer because it was just to much trying to do it on my phone" (P1)

**Related findings:** HE12, A05 (content clipped at 200% and 400% zoom, no layout for narrow screens)
